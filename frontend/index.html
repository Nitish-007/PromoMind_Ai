Regional Demand Rules for Karnataka & South India

Festive demand in retail is heavily localized. Demand for specific SKUs fluctuates dramatically by calendar dates.

🪔
Deepavali / Karthika Deepotsava:

Demand for Clay Deepas / Diyas, Pure Cow Ghee, and Cotton Wicks spikes by +140%. For non-festival months, Clay Deepa demand is near zero (<5 units/month).

🌾
Ayudha Puja & Vijayadashami:

Requires localized stock of Ash Gourd (Kumbalakai), Puffed Rice (Pori), Lemons, and Jaggery (Bella) (+60% surge for 3 days).
