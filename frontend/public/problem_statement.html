Section 09 // Equations & Proofs

Deterministic Mathematical & Algorithmic Foundations

1. Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) Formulation

The responsiveness of retail customer purchase volume to discount depth is formulated as:

$$\epsilon = - \frac{\% \Delta Q}{\% \Delta P} = - \frac{(Q_{\text{promo}} - Q_0) / Q_0}{(P_{\text{promo}} - P_0) / P_0}$$ $$Q_{\text{promo}} = Q_0 \cdot \left[ 1 - \epsilon \cdot \left( \frac{P_{\text{promo}} - P_0}{P_0} \right) \right]$$
Category Elasticity ($\epsilon$) Retail Dynamics Optimal Promotional Archetype
Staples & Edible Oils $\epsilon = 0.85$ Inelastic; essential daily household consumption. VOLUME_TIER_SLAB (Buy 5kg+, Save flat ₹25)
Packaged Snacks & Biscuits $\epsilon = 1.70$ Elastic; high impulse and brand substitution sensitivity. ANCHOR_MARGIN_BOOSTER (Pair with tea/coffee)
Festive Sweets & Dry Fruits $\epsilon = 2.10$ Highly Elastic; gifting budget allocation driven by deal perception. FESTIVE_HAMPER (Combo box with strikethrough MRP)

2. Holt-Winters Triple Exponential Smoothing

For inventory restock projections, the system utilizes Holt-Winters additive-multiplicative smoothing across 6 past weekly cycles:

$$\ell_t = \alpha (Y_t - s_{t-m}) + (1 - \alpha)(\ell_{t-1} + b_{t-1})$$ $$b_t = \beta (\ell_t - \ell_{t-1}) + (1 - \beta) b_{t-1}$$ $$\hat{Y}_{t+h} = (\ell_t + h b_t + s_{t+h-m}) \times \left( 1 + \frac{\text{EventSurgePct}}{100} \right)$$
