Data Science & Algorithms

Open-Source Math: Zero Black Boxes

Built completely on self-contained, deterministic Python mathematical foundations. Runs seamlessly offline or on micro cloud instances.

1. Constant Elasticity of Demand Model
%ΔQ = -ε · %ΔP  |  Q_sim = Q_base · (1 + lift) · (1 - discount)

Elasticity coefficients calibrated to Indian grocery categories:
• Staples & Oils: ε = 0.85 (Inelastic essential)
• Packaged Snacks: ε = 1.70 (Highly elastic impulse)
• Festive Sweets: ε = 2.10 (High sensitivity to gifting deals)
• Expiry Urgency Penalty: Automatically scales discount depth if shelf life < 30 days.

2. Double Exponential Smoothing (Holt's Linear)
L_t = α·Y_t + (1-α)·(L_{t-1} + T_{t-1})
T_t = β·(L_t - L_{t-1}) + (1-β)·T_{t-1}
F_{t+h} = L_t + h·T_t

Captures both historical baselines and progressive trends across 6-week sales curves, predicting exact 4-week SKU demand shifts without requiring heavy PyTorch/TensorFlow runtimes.
