## 🎬 Minute-by-Minute Script

### Part 1: The Hook & Modern Retail Crisis (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual**: Open `http://localhost:3000/presentation_deck.html` (Slides 1 & 2).
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Good afternoon, Judges! Independent retailers and supermarkets power the backbone of local commerce. But while quick-commerce and retail conglomerates deploy algorithmic dynamic pricing, independent merchants run blind discounts that bleed profits.*
  > *Retailers face three lethal problems: First, ₹2.8 Lakh trapped in slow-moving stock. Second, T-0 festival shocks where wholesalers inflate prices right before Diwali. Third, blind 20% discounts that destroy margins without moving volume.*
  > *We built **PromoMind AI** — an autonomous multi-agent platform combining open-source price elasticity forecasting, dynamic Vertex AI Sales Copilot reasoning, worldwide festival discovery, and Store Capital ROI intelligence."*

---

### Part 2: Screen 1 — Store Capital Investment & What-If ROI Simulator (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Visual**: Switch to `http://localhost:3000` (**Inventory / Stocks & Brands** tab).
- **Actions**:
  1. Point to the **Store Capital Investment & Returns / Profit Predictor** banner at the top.
  2. Click **"Open 'What-If' ROI Simulator"**.
  3. Drag the **Discount Depth Slider** to 18% and **Volume Lift** to 40%.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Here is the retailer's live command center. Rather than generic graphs, we show the merchant their exact financial health: **₹1.91 Lakh total capital invested**, **₹4.68 Lakh projected monthly turnover**, and **₹62,970 in dead capital trapped** in slow movers.*
  > *Watch this: The retailer opens our interactive **'What-If' ROI Simulator**. If they run an 18% festival discount expecting a 40% volume lift, the open-source Price Elasticity engine instantly computes: simulated turnover of ₹3.56 Lakh, ₹1.54 Lakh net profit, and accelerates capital turnover by **6 days faster** with a green **'PROFIT POSITIVE'** verdict!"*

---

### Part 3: Screen 2 — Dynamic AI Sales Copilot & Collateral Studio (1:00 - 1:40)
- **Visual**: Click top tab **"🤖 AI Sales Copilot"**.
- **Actions**:
  1. Ask the copilot: *"What products are failing in my store and how do I fix them with at least 22% margin?"*
  2. Point out the live reasoning generated via **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite on Vertex AI**.
  3. Show the **Cashier Counter Script**: 10-second verbal checkout pitch.
  4. Show the **Collateral Studio**: WhatsApp broadcast copy and print-ready high-contrast shelf posters.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Our AI Sales Copilot is 100% dynamic. It isn't canned text — it queries the store's active inventory velocities, solves mathematical margin constraints, and routes through Google Cloud Vertex AI using Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite.*
  > *Notice the output: It identifies our expiring products, calculates a clearance combo that preserves our 22% margin floor, and delivers a 10-second verbal cashier pitch for billing staff: 'Sir, Tea ke saath Britannia Cookies lene par flat ₹81 ki direct bachat hai!' Quoting exact rupee savings converts 2.3x higher than abstract percentages."*

---

### Part 4: Screen 3 — Worldwide Live AI Festival Discovery (1:40 - 2:20)
- **Visual**: Click top tab **"📅 Regional Festivals"**.
- **Actions**:
  1. Select or search a region: **"Karnataka & South India"** or type an international region like **"Australia"** or **"Spain"**.
  2. Click **"🔍 Live AI Search"** and highlight the live response with the `LIVE_VERTEX_SA_API` source badge.
  3. Point out the **3-Horizon Planning Deadlines**:
     - `📦 T-14d Advance Procurement Window`
     - `🚀 T-7d Customer Promo Launch`
     - `🧹 T+2d Clearance Deadline`
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Festive commerce is hyper-localized. For Deepavali in Karnataka, demand for Earthen Diyas and Pure Cow Ghee surges by **+140%**, while on regular days clay deepa sales drop to zero.*
  > *PromoMind AI searches upcoming events in real time using Vertex AI across Indian states and international markets like Australia and Spain, with transparent source indicators.*
  > *Our Multi-Horizon engine alerts merchants at **T-14 Days** to procure wholesale inventory before distributor rates inflate, launches early WhatsApp hampers at **T-7 Days**, and mandates a **T+2 Days** clearance window to avoid 100% inventory write-offs."*

---

### Part 5: Google Cloud Architecture & Zero-Downtime Resilience (2:20 - 3:00)
- **Visual**: Switch to **"🏪 Profile"** or show terminal logs showing **🟢 Google Cloud Firestore Live** & **Vertex AI**.
- **Spoken**:
  > *"Under the hood, PromoMind AI is built for enterprise resilience with a **Dual-Mode Google Cloud Firestore** adapter and **Vertex AI OAuth authentication** in us-central1. It runs offline with local JSON if the shop's internet drops, and auto-syncs to Google Cloud Firestore the moment connectivity returns.*
  > *The decoupled Next.js 15 frontend logs in within 100 milliseconds, and the entire test suite passes with 100% accuracy.*
  > *PromoMind AI turns neighborhood retailers into high-margin, predictive retail powerhouses. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"*
